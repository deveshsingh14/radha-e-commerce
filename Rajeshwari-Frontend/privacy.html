Your privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: we keep what we need to deliver your orders, and we count visits, searches and popular products anonymously to improve the shop. No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, and we never sell your data.

1. Your account and orders

When you register or order we store your name, email, phone number and delivery address, your cart, saved items and order history. We use these only to run your account, deliver your orders and send you order updates. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.

2. Anonymous shop statistics

To learn which products people like and what they look for, the shop records:

  • pages opened, products viewed, saved and added to the cart;
  • search words (anything that looks like an email address or phone number is thrown away) and the category you chose;
  • the delivery pincode you pick at checkout, or a pincode you tell us when your area isn't listed yet;
  • when the app is installed or notifications are turned on.

These records carry a random visitor ID kept in your browser (radha.visitor in local storage). It is an identifier, but it is not linked to your name, email, account or IP address. Clearing your browser data gives you a new one.

The 👁 number on a product is how many different visitors opened it (each visitor counts at most once a day). It is shown once a product reaches 10 and is approximate.

Opting out: if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, none of these statistics are recorded — the shop works the same. (That also means the numbers slightly undercount.)

4. Services we use

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages for the website, Render for the server, Neon for the database, Cloudflare): their systems process your requests and may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security.
  • Sentry (error reports): if a page or the server breaks, technical details of the error are sent so we can fix it. No account details are attached, and reset links and login tokens are removed first.
  • Cloudinary (product photos), Google Sign-In (only if you choose it) and your browser's push service (only if you turn on notifications).

5. Stored on your device

We don't use advertising cookies. The site keeps your login, the visitor ID, a copy of the catalogue for fast loading and a few display preferences in your browser's local storage.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent. You can edit your details on the Account page. For anything else, reply to any order email from us and we'll help.
